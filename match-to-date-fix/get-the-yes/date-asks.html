The Complete Match-to-Date FixThis folder

Get The Yes: Date Asks To Use

Use the ask that fits the conversation you have already had. Keep it short. Send it once. Then let her answer.

When The Chat Has Been Fun And Flirty

you are actually more fun to talk to than i expected. we should continue this over a drink this week
okay, i think we would get on dangerously well in person. let's get a drink this week
you are making a very convincing case for meeting you. drink this week?

When You Have Been Talking About Something Specific

you have made a strong case for [place / activity]. you might have to show me properly this week
this debate is clearly better in person. let's settle it over a drink this week
i still need to hear the rest of that story. let's continue it over coffee this weekend
you clearly know more about [topic] than i do. teach me over a drink this week?

When You Want To Be Simple And Direct

i have enjoyed talking to you. let's get a drink this week
you seem fun. are you free for a drink this week?
let's take this off the app. coffee or a drink this week?

If She Says Yes

Keep it simple. Do not keep chatting for another hour.

perfect. let's get it in the calendar

Then go to Put The Date In The Calendar.

If She Says She Is Busy But Suggests Another Day

That is still a yes. She is helping you make the plan.

Her:

i'm busy this week but saturday could work

You:

saturday works. let's sort the details

Then go to Put The Date In The Calendar.

If She Says "Maybe"

Do not try to talk her into it. Keep it easy and leave the next move with her.

no stress. let me know if you decide you are up for it

If She Says She Is Busy But Gives No Alternative

Do not ask "when are you free?" That turns a vague answer into a negotiation she did not offer.

no worries. let me know when you have a gap

Then leave it.

If She Says She Does Not Know Her Schedule

no problem. give me a shout when you know what your week looks like

Then leave it.

If She Says No

Keep it clean.

all good. nice talking to you

Do not ask why. Do not try a different date idea. Do not make it awkward.

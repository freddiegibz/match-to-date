﻿# Get The Reply: Messages To Use

Pick one message. Do not send two openers. Do not explain the hook until she replies.

Copy And Paste

We have tried hundreds of openers. These are the ones that consistently proved best at getting a reply.

ur cute but you look like trouble
hey [name], you know what's interesting about your pictures?
can i be honest?

If she says yes, reply:

you're just my type
you look like a bad influence
i cannot decide if you look sweet or just very good at pretending to be
quick question, are you always this hard to read or is it just the pictures?

Fill In The Blanks

Use these when there is something in her profile that gives you an angle. Replace everything in brackets before you send it.

okay, the [photo / prompt / detail] tells me you are either [playful assumption] or [playful assumption]. which one is it?
i have a theory about the [photo / prompt / detail], but i need you to confirm it first
that [photo / prompt / detail] is giving me [playful assumption] energy. fair?
i cannot work out whether the [photo / prompt / detail] makes you look more [positive trait] or more [playful trait]
be honest, was the [photo / prompt / detail] your idea or did someone make you do it?
i feel like there is a story behind the [photo / prompt /detail]. how wrong am i?
the [photo / prompt / detail] is a very bold choice. i respect it
you seem like the kind of person who would [playful assumption based on profile detail]. tell me i am wrong

Quick Examples

Profile detail: a photo of her jumping into the sea

okay, the jumping-in-the-sea photo tells me you are either fun on holiday or an absolute liability. which one is it?

Profile detail: a prompt saying she loves spicy margaritas

i have a theory about the spicy margarita answer, but i need you to confirm it first

Profile detail: a photo with her dog

i cannot work out whether the dog makes you look sweeter or more like you know exactly how to get your own way

Before You Send It

  • [ ] Does it make her curious or give her something to play along with?
  • [ ] Is it light and flattering?
  • [ ] Do you know what you will say when she asks what you mean?
  • [ ] Does it sound like you?
